                The road to war, 1919 to 1939.

                What this lesson is really about

                Hitler wanted living space in the east and took it step by step. Britain and France gave way to keep the peace, until 1939.

                CAPS covers the expansion "very briefly". Keep it to cause and effect.

                They should leave able to say

                • What meant, and how Hitler expanded Germany from 1936 to 1939.
                • What appeasement was, and why Britain and France chose it.
                • Why war broke out in 1939, and who was on each side.

                The story, step by step

                Why Britain and France appeased

                Memories of the First World War

                Around 900 000 British soldiers had died. Few people wanted another war, and leaders knew it.

                Not ready to fight

                Britain's army was small and its air defences were still being built. Military advisers warned it could not win a war in 1938.

                seemed unfair

                Some thought Germany had been treated too harshly, so taking back German-speaking areas seemed almost reasonable.

                Fear of communism

                Some British leaders saw the Soviet Union as the bigger threat, and a strong Germany as a barrier against it.

                Money and the empire

                After the Depression, rearming was expensive. Dominions such as Canada and South Africa did not want war over Czechoslovakia.

                Why it failed

                Hitler's aims had no limit

                He wanted living space in eastern Europe, not just to unite German speakers. March 1939 proved it.

                Each success made him bolder

                Every time Britain and France backed down, Hitler concluded they would never fight.

                It weakened possible allies

                Czechoslovakia lost its border defences and arms factories. The Soviet Union, left out of Munich, made its own deal with Hitler.

                What historians argue

                "Guilty men"

                A 1940 pamphlet blamed Chamberlain and his ministers. For years appeasement was seen as weak and naive.

                "Buying time"

                Later historians argued Munich gave Britain a year to rearm. Fighter planes and radar grew a great deal by 1939.

                Learners who weigh both views get further than those who pick one.

                What learners usually get wrong

                "Chamberlain was just a coward."

                Push them to explain why a careful leader might choose peace. Then ask what he got wrong.

                "Munich gave Hitler all of Czechoslovakia."

                It gave him the only. He took the rest himself, six months later.

                "Britain could easily have stopped him."

                Maybe in 1936. By 1938 its leaders believed it couldn't. Ask: what did they know at the time?

                Questions they might ask you

                Why didn't the Czechs fight?

                Alone they stood little chance, and they were not even invited to Munich. Their leaders gave in under heavy pressure.

                Where was America?

                The United States stayed out of European affairs in the 1930s. It did not join the war until December 1941.

                Did people support Chamberlain?

                Many did. Crowds cheered him after Munich. A smaller group, including Winston Churchill, warned it would fail.
